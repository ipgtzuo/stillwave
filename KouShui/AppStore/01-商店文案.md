**描述**
```
叩音，是一片会记住你的水面。

用一根手指轻轻叩下去，水面荡开一层连续的波纹，一个音落下。连敲成句，停下来几秒，水会把刚才那一句更轻、更远地还给你一次，然后归于静。

不打分，不计数排行，也没有课程。它只给压力上来的那几十秒，一个不用看屏幕也能做完的小动作。

· 有曲调的随机：宫商角徵羽按三分损益律定音，级进为主、每句有自己的起伏轮廓、句尾收得住，怎么敲都成调，但每句都不一样。
· 四种心境：澄明 / 悠远 / 轻扬 / 幽思，决定这一句是收敛、开阔、上行还是低回。
· 五套景：墨池 / 星潭 / 宣纸 / 雨夜 / 松雾。水一直在极缓地流动，敲下去的瞬间它会屏息让位。
· 五行墨符：每次落指浮起一笔手绘墨符，形状与这个音的性格对应。
· 落点即乐器：左右决定声像，上下决定远近与明暗。
· 水会回想：一句敲完静下来，它把那一句还给你一次，然后才真正安静。

在 Apple Watch 上，声音默认关闭。曲调改由最轻的触觉节奏承载——音越高，两下轻拍靠得越近；放下手腕、屏幕熄灭后，这一句仍然能敲完。开会、通勤、夜里，不用戴耳机，也不用担心突然出声。iPhone 上则可以用 Core Haptics 真正调出柔软的触感，四种材质可选。

不收集任何数据，不联网，没有账号、广告或订阅。想安静一会儿的时候，敲一下就好。

「课虚无以责有，叩寂寞而求音。」—— 晋 · 陆机《文赋》
```

**Description**
```
Stillwave is a pool of water that remembers what you just played.

Tap anywhere with one finger. A continuous wave opens across the surface and a note falls. Keep tapping and the notes form a phrase. Stop, and a few seconds later the water gives that phrase back to you — quieter, farther away — before it settles into stillness.

No scores. No streaks. No courses. Just a small thing you can finish in under a minute, without looking at the screen.

- Melodic, not random: the five tones of the Chinese pentatonic scale (gong, shang, jue, zhi, yu) are tuned by the ancient 3:2 ratio system. Each phrase wanders by small steps along its own contour and resolves at the end, so every phrase is different and none of them sound wrong.
- Four moods: Clear, Distant, Lifting, Pensive — each changes the register, direction and length of a phrase.
- Five scenes: Ink Pool, Star Pond, Rice Paper, Rain Night, Pine Mist. The surface is always moving very slowly, and it briefly holds its breath when you tap.
- Five drawn ink glyphs: every tap lifts a hand-drawn mark whose shape matches the character of that tone.
- Where you tap is part of the instrument: left to right shifts the sound, up and down shifts distance and brightness.
- The water remembers: one echo, then silence.

On Apple Watch, sound is off by default. The melody moves into the lightest haptic rhythm instead — higher notes sit closer together — and it keeps working after you lower your wrist and the screen goes dark. In a meeting, on a train, late at night: no headphones required, no sudden audio. On iPhone, Core Haptics makes the touch itself adjustable, with four softness presets.

No data collected. No network. No account, no ads, no subscription.

Tap once, whenever you want a moment of quiet.
```

## 命名说明（上架前仍需查重）

中文名定为 **叩音**（kòu yīn）：直接取自陆机《文赋》「课虚无以责有，叩寂寞而求音」的末二字——静里敲一下，才有音。既保留「叩」的动作，也点出五音与触觉乐谱，且没有旧名那种被听错的风险。

- 英文名 **Stillwave** 不变：still（静）+ wave（波）对应连续波场的视觉；中文说「音」，英文说「静与波」，一体两面。
- 副标「一叩一应 · 腕上的片刻安静」不变，正好呼应「叩」与「音」的一来一往。
- 图标概念名「一叩 / One Knock」只用于设计说明，不是商店名。
- 名字集中在 `Shared/KouShuiModel.swift` 的 `appName` / `appDisplayName`、两个 `Info.plist` 的 `CFBundleDisplayName`、`AppStore/metadata.json` 与 ASC 名称字段；再改名时同步这几处即可全站生效。
- **必须在 App Store Connect 正式查重**，并检查商标与同名 App。本文件不能替代查重。
